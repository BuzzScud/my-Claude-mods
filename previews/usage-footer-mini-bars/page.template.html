Why it shrinks so early today. Claude Code's own text on the left (“⏵⏵ bypass permissions on (shift+tab to cycle) · esc to interrupt”) gets 66 of your 120 columns, so the row has about 51. Today's tiles pad every name to 5 columns, every percent to 4, and put “ │ ” between tiles. So at 120 the lamps are gone, and with a background shell running one gauge is hidden behind “+1”.

Mini bars keep the same parts in a smaller space: the name, a short smooth bar (each cell fills in eighths, so 2 cells still show 16 steps), a 3-column percent, then the reset time when there is room. At 120, all four gauges keep their bars (46 columns), even with a background shell. Same colours, same order, same motion as today. “week” becomes “wk”, and 100% shows as “max”.

Kept from today

  • Always one row, never wider than the room the left text leaves, so Claude Code never pushes it onto a second row.
  • The same size whether Claude is working or not. Room is kept for “· esc to interrupt”, and for wider left text once seen (a background shell).
  • One order (ctx, 5h, wk, fable), and every gauge at the same step.
  • Narrower never shows more. First the bars shorten (8 → 4 → 2 cells), then the times go, then the bars. Only after that does a gauge go, the calmest first, with +n.
  • Colours: ctx blue, limits green, yellow when going faster than the clock, red at 90% or more. On the track, grey is time gone that you did not use, dark is still to come, and dark red is past where the context compacts.
  • Motion: a light runs along the bars while Claude works; the last cell breathes at rest; a reply's new part flashes in.

What changes

  • Lamps (▊ with gaps) become smooth bars on a dark track, cut to the eighth of a cell.
  • No padding: names at their own length, a 3-column percent, 2 spaces between gauges instead of “ │ ”.
  • “week” → “wk”. 100% and over shows as max, in red.
  • Only row.ts and a name in limits.ts change. The Fable reading, the room rule, /limits on | off and the timing are untouched.

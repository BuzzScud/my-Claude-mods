Usage footer — two new designs

Every row below is drawn by the mod's own drawing code, cell for cell, in the colours your terminal shows. Both new designs fix the three things you picked: the bars are bigger, the reset times stay, and the colour says how full a gauge is. They also shorten (shift+tab to cycle) to free room. On 29 Oct 2026 both were tried live as /limits a and /limits b, and B was picked from this page. It shipped as 0.7.0.

Today · Mini bars 0.6.0

Colour follows pace, so 79% shows green. At 140 columns it gets only 71 columns, so the bars shrink to 1–2 cells and the times drop.

A · Long bars tried, not kept

Bars up to 10 cells, coloured by how full they are. A │ notch marks how far through its window you are, so a bar past its notch is running ahead of the clock. Times stay down to ~125 columns.

B · Bar tiles picked · 0.7.0

Each gauge is a tile whose background fills like a bar, with the name, percent and reset time printed on it. The bar is the whole tile (16–24 cells), and every time stays at 120 columns. No pace notch.
